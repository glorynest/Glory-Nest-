Bible Verse

1 John 1:9 — Confession and Forgiveness

“If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just and will forgive us our sins and purify us from all unrighteousness.”
1 John 1:9

A moment to reflect

God invites us to come before Him honestly, confess our sins, and trust in His faithfulness. This verse offers hope that our failures do not have to keep us from seeking His forgiveness and walking in His truth.

What 1 John 1:9 Means

First John 1:9 teaches that God is faithful and just to forgive us when we confess our sins. Confession means being honest with God rather than hiding or making excuses for our wrong choices. His forgiveness offers a fresh start and helps us grow in our relationship with Him.

How to Apply This Verse to Your Life

Take a quiet moment to reflect on your choices. Admit what went wrong, ask God for forgiveness, and consider what you can do differently. If your actions hurt someone, seek to make things right where possible. Let God's forgiveness encourage you to grow with honesty, humility, and hope.

A Prayer for Forgiveness and a Fresh Start

Heavenly Father, help me come before You with an honest heart. Give me the courage to admit my mistakes and the humility to seek Your forgiveness. Thank You for Your faithfulness and mercy. Guide me to make better choices, repair the harm I have caused where possible, and walk in Your truth each day. In Jesus' name, Amen.
