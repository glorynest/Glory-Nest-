# Glory Nest

Publish-ready starter for a Christian Scripture and prayer website.

## Included
- Responsive homepage
- Bible verses and topic filters
- Search
- Browser-saved favorites
- Verse of the Day
- Prayer library
- Scripture poster gallery
- Individual SEO-friendly verse pages
- About, Privacy, Terms, and Contact pages
- robots.txt and sitemap.xml
- favicon and web manifest

## Before publishing
1. Replace `https://glorynest.github.io/Glory-Nest/` in canonical URLs and sitemap if you choose another domain.
2. Replace the placeholder contact email in `pages/contact.html`.
3. Review the Privacy Policy and Terms for your actual business, analytics, cookies, advertising, and contact setup.
4. Verify that every Bible translation quotation and image used on the final site is legally usable.

## Publish
Upload the files to your chosen static host (GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages, Netlify, etc.).

## Google
After the site is live, verify the domain in Google Search Console and submit:
https://YOUR-DOMAIN/sitemap.xml

Google recommends people-first content and good page experience. Search indexing is not instant.
