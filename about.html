About Glory Nest

Faith, hope, and God's Word.

Glory Nest is a peaceful online space created to help people pause, read Scripture, pray honestly, and find encouragement for the day ahead.

What you'll find here

Bible verses organized by common life situations, simple prayers for difficult moments, and scripture-inspired posters designed to keep God's Word close.

Our purpose

We want every visit to feel like a quiet place to breathe, reflect, and turn your heart toward God.

Meet the Founder

Subham Mahanandia
Founder of Glory Nest
Son of Mr. Ranjit Kumar Mahanandia

By the grace of God, Glory Nest was created from a simple dream: to build a peaceful online space where people can find hope in God's Word, comfort through prayer, and encouragement for everyday life.

My name is Subham, and I built this project with faith, dedication, and a desire to share God's message of hope with others. I believe that even a small beginning can grow into something meaningful when guided by faith, patience, and sincere effort.

I am grateful to my parents for being part of my journey. My hope is to keep learning, work hard, and grow Glory Nest into something that helps people and makes my family proud.

My prayer is that everyone who visits Glory Nest finds a little more peace, a little more hope, and encouragement to keep moving forward with faith.

“Unless the Lord builds the house, the builders labor in vain.” — Psalm 127:1

With faith and gratitude,
Subham
