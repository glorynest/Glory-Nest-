Bible Verse

Isaiah 43:18–19 — God Is Doing a New Thing

“Forget the former things; do not dwell on the past. See, I am doing a new thing!”
Isaiah 43:18–19

A moment to reflect

Isaiah 43:18–19 offers hope when life feels stuck or the future seems uncertain. God invites His people to look forward with faith and recognize that new possibilities can emerge even in difficult seasons.

What Isaiah 43:18–19 Means

In this passage, God promises to do a new thing for His people, making a way in the wilderness and rivers in the desert. In its original context, this message offered hope for God's people and His work of restoration. Today, it can remind us to trust God's faithfulness while remaining open to His guidance through uncertain times.

How to Apply This Verse to Your Life

If you're facing a difficult season, acknowledge your feelings without losing hope. Reflect on what you can learn from the past, pray for wisdom, and take one practical step toward a healthier future. A new beginning may start with a small decision to trust God and keep moving forward.

A Prayer for New Beginnings

Heavenly Father, when I feel lost or uncertain, remind me of Your faithfulness. Help me release the things I cannot change and give me courage to take the next step. Open my heart to Your guidance, strengthen my hope, and teach me to recognize the new opportunities You place before me. Walk with me through every wilderness and help me trust You along the way. In Jesus' name, Amen.
