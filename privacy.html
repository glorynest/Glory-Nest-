Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Glory Nest is a Scripture and prayer app designed to support Bible reading, prayer, gratitude, and reflection. We respect your privacy and aim to keep your personal journal information on your device.

Information We Collect

Glory Nest does not require an account. The current version does not include an account-based cloud storage system or cloud synchronization for journal entries.

Information Stored on Your Device

The app may store saved Bible verses, favorites, prayer journal entries, prayer statuses such as “Praying” or “Answered,” gratitude notes, reading streak information, and app preferences in your browser's local storage.

This information is not sent to a Glory Nest server for account-based storage or synchronization in the current version. Local storage is not encrypted or backed up by Glory Nest. Anyone with access to your device or browser profile may be able to view stored information. Please avoid entering highly sensitive personal information.

Exporting Journal Entries

The app includes a feature to export prayer journal entries as a local JSON file. You are responsible for keeping any exported file secure.

Offline Use

Glory Nest includes offline support for certain app features and files. Some external content, such as remote images, may not be available offline.

Third-Party Services

The current version does not include an account system or cloud synchronization. If analytics, advertising, payment services, or other third-party services are added in the future, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant data practices.

Children's Privacy

Glory Nest does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account system. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of the app and the information they choose to enter.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as Glory Nest changes. The latest version will be published on the Glory Nest website with its updated date.
